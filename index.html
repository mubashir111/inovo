About INOVO

A contemporary practice shaping the spaces people remember.

INOVO Developers is an architecture and interior design practice based in Kozhikode, Kerala. Founded in 2017, the studio works across residential, commercial and hospitality environments, bringing architecture, interiors and spatial experience together.

VISIONTo create spaces with lasting architectural and emotional value.
NICHEContemporary, design-led architecture and refined interiors.
DESIGN PHILOSOPHYClarity of form. Richness of material. Precision in every detail.
2017The practice is established.
TodayWork across residential, commercial and hospitality sectors.
